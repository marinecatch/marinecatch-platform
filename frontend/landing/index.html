How MarineCatch Works
From landing site to buyer's kitchen. Fully digital. Fully traceable.
1

Fisher Logs Catch

Fisher dials *384*71253# or sends a WhatsApp message to log species, weight, and landing site. Works on any phone, zero data required.

2

Inventory Created

Catch is logged in real-time to MarineCatch inventory with full traceability including species, location, gear type, BMU reference, IUU risk assessment.

3

Buyer Orders

Hotels, restaurants and processors browse available stock and place orders via WhatsApp, the web platform, or institutional LPO flow.

4

Payment & Delivery

M-Pesa payment confirmed. Cold-chain logistics dispatched. IoT temperature monitoring throughout. Fisher paid directly to M-Pesa on delivery.

Platform Features
Built for African Fisheries
Infrastructure that works in low-connectivity environments, on feature phones, and across Kenya's seafood corridors.
📱

WhatsApp & USSD

Fishers and buyers interact through WhatsApp and USSD. No app download. No data required for USSD. Works on any handset.

💚

Instant M-Pesa Payouts

Fishers receive payment directly to their M-Pesa the moment delivery is confirmed. No cash handling. Full transaction record.

🔍

Full Traceability

Every lot traced from catch to consumer. IUU risk assessment, BMU verification, cold chain monitoring. Export-ready compliance records.

❄️

Cold Chain IoT

Real-time temperature and humidity monitoring during transit. Automatic breach detection and alerts. Built for Kenya's seafood corridors.

📋

Institutional LPO Flow

Hotels and processors submit purchase orders digitally. Invoice generation, Net 7/14/30 payment terms, automated reconciliation.

🌍

ESG & Sustainability

Species sustainability profiles, fisher impact data, gender participation tracking. Ready for blue economy fund reporting and eco-labelling.
