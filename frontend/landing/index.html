How MarineCatch Works
From landing site to buyer's kitchen. Fully digital. Fully traceable.
1

Fisher Logs Catch

Species, weight, landing site — logged via WhatsApp or USSD in under a minute. Any phone, zero data required.

2

Inventory Created

Catch appears in real-time inventory with full traceability — species, location, gear type, and BMU reference.

Buyer Orders

Payment & Delivery

M-Pesa payment confirmed instantly. Fisher paid the moment delivery is confirmed.
